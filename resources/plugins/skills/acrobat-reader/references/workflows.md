# Acrobat Reader workflow notes

## Review workflow
1) Open the PDF and capture the review objective.
2) Summarize comment threads, unresolved issues, and page references.
3) Export or hand off a review-ready file with comments preserved if requested.

## Packet assembly workflow
1) Confirm the source file set and target order.
2) Combine files, then reorder, rotate, or remove pages as needed.
3) Re-open the final packet and confirm page count, section transitions, and bookmarks.

## Compare workflow
1) Identify the baseline and revised PDFs.
2) Compare versions and summarize meaningful additions, removals, and formatting drift.
3) Deliver a short delta summary plus the final compared artifact if requested.

## Export workflow
1) Confirm target format and fidelity expectations.
2) Export once, then inspect the result for missing pages, broken fonts, or layout drift.
3) Keep the PDF source untouched until the export passes review.
