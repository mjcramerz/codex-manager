# Acrobat forms and signature delivery checklist

## Before handoff
- confirm the expected signer order
- confirm whether the output should stay fillable or be flattened
- confirm the exact filenames for working, signer-ready, and archival copies

## Validation
- open the final file and verify fields, signatures, and page layout
- verify required fields are not missing or duplicated
- verify the final packet is readable without Acrobat-specific editing context

## Handoff note
- list any manual signature or submission steps still required
- list any archival or record-keeping copy that was produced
