# Acrobat forms and sign workflows

## Fillable form preparation
1) Inspect the source PDF for required fields and missing labels.
2) Prepare or verify the field structure.
3) Save a working copy before any flattening or final export.

## Fill and sign workflow
1) Confirm the signer inputs and required completion order.
2) Fill the document or prepare signer-ready placeholders.
3) Re-open the result and verify expected fill/sign behavior.

## Archival workflow
1) Confirm whether the user wants a flattened archival copy.
2) Produce the archival file separately from the working copy.
3) Verify the archival copy is readable, complete, and no longer editable when requested.
